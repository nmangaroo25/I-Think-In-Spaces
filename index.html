About Us

NYC & NYS Certified MBE Architectural Consultancy

I Think In Spaces, LLC is a New York–certified MBE architectural consultancy specializing in high-fidelity pre-construction and technical documentation. We bridge the gap between conceptual design and field execution by providing a rigorous, interdisciplinary approach to floor plans, coordinated layouts, and existing conditions (as-built) documentation.

Led by a principal with over 15 years of experience in multidisciplinary project delivery, our firm leverages advanced BIM and CAD workflows to provide prime consultants and contractors with sophisticated model management and constructability analysis[cite: 2]. At I Think In Spaces, we do not simply produce drawings; we ensure project accuracy, interdisciplinary alignment, and regulatory compliance through every phase of the project lifecycle[cite: 1, 2].
